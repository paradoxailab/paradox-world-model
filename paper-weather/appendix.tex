\paragraph{Hybrid designs.}
H1 adds the bias-corrected forecasts as inputs (v0.3 used IFS 0.25 and AIFS runs at least 48 h old; from v0.4 on,
the four models' newest runs issued before the forecast start). H2 also makes their mean the base (v0.4). H3 adds
cloud cover, shortwave radiation, dew point, sea-level pressure and wind direction from the same runs (v0.5). Each
is trained on the league's best pure setting. Designs are compared on one seed in the order H1, H2, H3, and a design
replaces the current choice only if it is better by more than $2\sigma$.

\paragraph{Training.}
\begin{itemize}
\item AdamW, learning rate $1.5\times10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch 32 origins, 6{,}000 steps.
\item Linear warm-up over 300 steps, then cosine decay to 10\% of the peak rate.
\item Validation every 500 steps; the best checkpoint is kept.
\item Mixed precision on a single Kaggle GPU.
\item Training origins are every hour; validation and test origins are every 6 hours.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Rolling fine-tuning (v0.7, rejected).}
Every 168 hours, each final model is fine-tuned for 500 steps at learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$, using only hours
observed before that week, with half of the batches drawn from the last 720 hours.

\paragraph{Splits.}
Training, validation and test periods are given in \S\ref{sec:data}. Normalisation statistics come from the
training period only. On top of each league's station selection, the pipeline keeps a station only if at least 25\% of its training-period temperature hours and of its
test-period target hours are observed, a rule fixed before any scoring.
