\section{Leagues and data}\label{sec:data}

Every league forecasts 2 m temperature (°C), relative humidity (\%) and 10 m wind speed (m/s) for the next 24
hours. Forecast origins are every 6 hours, and only observations up to the hour before the origin are used.
Stations and splits were fixed by rules written before any score was seen (Table~\ref{tab:leagues}).

\paragraph{METAR (airports).}
These are routine airport reports from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive. The candidates were every Indian
station within 600 km of Delhi Palam and below 1,000 m, of which at most 16 could be kept, and a station was kept
only if at least 60\% of its hours had a temperature in both periods. Six airports qualified: Delhi Palam, Jaipur,
Amritsar, Lucknow, Udaipur and Bhopal. Each hourly value is the report nearest the top of the hour, within
$\pm$15 min.

\paragraph{WMO (NOAA ISD).}
These are quality-controlled synoptic and METAR records from NOAA's Integrated Surface Database \citep{smith2011isd}.
We took the nearest stations within 500 km of Delhi and below 1,000 m with at least 10\% coverage, up to 24.
Of these 24, 20 passed the model's coverage rule, including Delhi Safdarjung and Palam. Many report only every 3 hours, so many hours are missing,
and scores use observed hours only. NOAA's Indian records end on 2025-08-24, which fixes the test period.

\paragraph{CPCB (Delhi air-quality stations).}
These are the weather sensors at Delhi's air-quality monitoring stations. Stations were located by name with
OpenStreetMap, and 25 of 37 passed the coverage rule. Runs of identical readings of 12 h or longer are masked as
stuck. The sensors are not sited to WMO standards: many are on rooftops, and wind is often sheltered (19.6\% of wind
hours are missing). The league therefore tests forecasts of what these sensors will read, which matters for
air-quality work. It is not a test of free-air weather.

\paragraph{Archived operational forecasts.}
Open-Meteo's Previous Runs API \citep{openmeteo} stores the forecasts that operational models actually issued. It
gives each model's latest run and its runs from 1 and 2 days earlier, which we read at each station's nearest
0.05° point. The models are ECMWF AIFS \citep{lang2024aifs}, ECMWF IFS at 9 km and at 0.25°, DWD ICON, NOAA GFS and
NOAA AIGFS (GraphCast-based). AIGFS has a short archive and no humidity, so it appears in the METAR
league only.
