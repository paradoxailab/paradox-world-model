Compact world models for the physical world: weather, air quality and traffic. Trained from scratch on real sensor data, tested in public, every loss reported. Independent AI lab from India · Aditya Kumar & Anupam Singh.

Where we stand (November 2026)

TestResult
🌦️ Weather, 709 stations worldwide, 24 h WIN 100 / 100 against ten zero-shot opponents: nine foundation models (TimesFM 2.5 / 3.0, Chronos-2 uni / multi, Chronos-Bolt, TiRex, Sundial, Time-MoE, TTM) and persistence
🛰️ Weather + global forecasts (Atlas-100M-H2), 178 stations worldwide, 2025 Lower error than raw and bias-corrected ECMWF IFS and NOAA GFS on temperature, dewpoint and wind (11–34%). Loses to bias-corrected IFS on sea-level pressure. GraphCast comparison pending.
🇮🇳 Weather, all 68 Indian stations Level with the foundation models (WIN 19, TIE 74, LOSS 7)
🌫️ PM2.5, all-India CPCB WIN against 7 of 9 foundation models; ties TimesFM 3.0
🚦 Traffic, six road networks Atlas-100M-TXL2: no loss to any foundation model (18 / 12 / 0); still behind the best trained specialists
